# Requirement Intelligence Assistant (RIA) — Video Voiceover & Audio Script

**Total Estimated Duration:** ~4 to 5 minutes  
**Pacing:** Moderate, clear, and confident  
**Tone:** Professional Product Walkthrough / Demo  

---

## 🎬 Section 1: Introduction & Executive Overview
*(Estimated Time: ~30s)*

**[Visual Cue: Video starts on the RIA Home Dashboard with clean branding]**

> **Voiceover:**  
> "Welcome to this walkthrough of the **Requirement Intelligence Assistant**, or **RIA**—an AI-powered assistant built for Business Analysts, Product Owners, and QA leads.
> 
> In enterprise projects, requirements often come from scattered, contradictory sources: meeting recordings, early BRDs, and policy documents. RIA bridges this gap by automatically ingesting these materials, detecting contradictions, and transforming them into validated functional requirements, agile user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria, and QA test cases. 
> 
> Let's walk through the complete end-to-end flow."

---

## 🎬 Section 2: Home Dashboard & Session Setup
*(Estimated Time: ~30s)*

**[Visual Cue: Cursor hovers over Active Session card, then shows session switching and history table]**

> **Voiceover:**  
> "We begin on the **Home Dashboard**. 
> 
> Here, you can see your active session along with its creation date and quick navigation shortcuts. RIA allows you to organize your work by client, release, or sprint. You can seamlessly switch between active sessions, create a brand-new workspace with custom naming, or clone an earlier run to compare requirement deltas. 
> 
> Notice how clean the interface is—backend configurations and model details are tucked away so the focus stays entirely on requirement governance. 
> 
> Let's head over to **Prepare & Run**."

---

## 🎬 Section 3: Document Ingestion (Prepare & Run)
*(Estimated Time: ~40s)*

**[Visual Cue: Navigating to Prepare & Run; showing uploaded documents table DOC-001, DOC-002, DOC-003]**

> **Voiceover:**  
> "In the **Prepare & Run** module, we stage our project documentation.
> 
> RIA supports heterogeneous multi-format ingestion. You can drag and drop meeting transcripts, Word documents, Markdown specs, PDFs, or even subtitle tracks. 
> 
> In this demo, we have ingested three source documents: a formal Business Requirements Document, a company policy document, and an informal stakeholder meeting transcript.
> 
> Before any AI analysis takes place, RIA automatically parses the content, segments it, and redacts sensitive personally identifiable information—such as customer names, phone numbers, and emails—ensuring full privacy and compliance."

---

## 🎬 Section 4: AI Pipeline Execution
*(Estimated Time: ~35s)*

**[Visual Cue: Scrolling down to Analysis Options, selecting Reconciliation mode, pointing to Run Analysis button]**

> **Voiceover:**  
> "Next, we configure our analysis options.
> 
> We can choose between **Cross-document reconciliation**—which compares spoken meeting agreements against formal written specs—or **Transcript-only mode** for fast grooming sessions. We also select our analysis depth.
> 
> When we click **Run Analysis**, RIA triggers a deterministic 5-stage pipeline:
> First, it extracts atomic statements.
> Second, it deduces normalized functional requirements.
> Third, it flags cross-document conflicts and gaps.
> And finally, it generates agile user stories and QA test scenarios.
> 
> Once the run completes, let's proceed to **Review & Analysis** to inspect the results."

---

## 🎬 Section 5: Review & Analysis Workbench
*(Estimated Time: ~45s)*

**[Visual Cue: Navigating to Review & Analysis; highlighting status metric cards especially 'Confirm Requirement', then scrolling the requirements table]**

> **Voiceover:**  
> "This is the **Review & Analysis** workbench, where Business Analysts review and validate what the AI synthesized.
> 
> At the top, you'll notice dynamic metric cards. In particular, we have a dedicated **Confirm Requirement** card showing items that are confirmed and ready for delivery, alongside Approved, Needs Clarification, and Conflict Detected metrics.
> 
> In the table below, each requirement displays its unique ID, functional category, title, source document references, and review status. 
> 
> As a BA, you have complete control: you can approve requirements, flag ambiguous items, edit descriptions inline, or request changes with full audit tracking."

---

## 🎬 Section 6: Clarification Email & Conflict Resolution
*(Estimated Time: ~40s)*

**[Visual Cue: Clicking 'Draft clarification email', showing the panel expand with the single consolidated Conflicting Requirements table, then pointing to 'Open Directly in Outlook']**

> **Voiceover:**  
> "One of RIA's most powerful features is automated stakeholder reconciliation.
> 
> When we click **Draft clarification email**, RIA identifies concrete contradictions between what was agreed in the meeting versus what the written BRD specifies.
> 
> All contradictions are consolidated into a single clean table—eliminating duplicate matrices. It details the conflict title, the exact conflicting values and their source files, the business and QA impact, and the precise question to ask the product owner.
> 
> With one click on **Open Directly in Outlook**, RIA uses local Windows automation to launch a formatted draft email right inside Outlook, complete with styled HTML tables and suggested recipients."

---

## 🎬 Section 7: User Stories & QA Test Scenarios
*(Estimated Time: ~50s)*

**[Visual Cue: Navigating to Stories & Tests; showing the 4-section layout of US-001, expanding Acceptance Requirement & QA tests]**

> **Voiceover:**  
> "Now let's move to **Stories & Tests**, where approved requirements are converted into technical backlogs.
> 
> Notice the strict **4-section architecture** of every user story:
> Number one: **Description**—the agile persona narrative: As a user, I want, So that.
> Number two: **Path or Navigation**—specifying exactly where in the application the feature lives.
> Number three: **Functional Requirement**—displaying the complete, unabridged requirement text directly on the card so engineers never lose business context.
> And number four: **Acceptance Requirement & QA Tests**—expanding this shows Gherkin Given-When-Then criteria with quick-edit popovers, followed by automated QA scenarios grouped into Happy Path and Edge Cases with numbered test steps."

---

## 🎬 Section 8: Jira Backlog Push
*(Estimated Time: ~35s)*

**[Visual Cue: Clicking the 'Jira push' tab; showing connection test and issue selection]**

> **Voiceover:**  
> "Switching to the **Jira push** tab, RIA seamlessly connects to your team's backlog.
> 
> You can test the live REST API connection with one click, filter for sprint-ready approved stories, preview the exact JSON payload, and push stories directly into Jira. 
> 
> Once pushed, RIA records the created Jira issue keys with direct hyperlinks right into your project board."

> And third, a **Jira CSV** for teams using bulk backlog imports."
